Compliance by design

Our publishing commitments

This page is a concept proposal, not a representation that a live affiliate or age-verification integration is currently operating.

Licensed promotional materials

Only images and banners authorised by the advertiser or rights holder. No scraping, copied previews or unauthorised embeds.

Adult performer protections

No depiction of minors or anyone appearing underage. Verified adult status and consent are mandatory for any real-person promotional creative.

Location and age controls

Restricted offers stay disabled until the applicable jurisdictional requirements and appropriate age-assurance methods have been established and tested.

Privacy, review and takedown

Minimal visitor data, clear affiliate disclosure, proactive creative review, advertiser link checks and a takedown process before launch.
